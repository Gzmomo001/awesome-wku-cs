# Welcome to Awesome WKU CS

A curated collection of resources for WKU Computer Science students.

Here, you can find useful materials, course resources, books, and learning references to support your CS journey at WKU.

## After-Class / Personal Notes

Some notes and resources may be personal study materials from the original contributors and may not correspond to official WKU lectures.

Please keep WKU-specific course content under `course/`. Personal or cross-topic notes should be placed in `afterClass/`.
